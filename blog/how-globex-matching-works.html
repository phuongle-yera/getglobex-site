How GetGlobex matching works

GetGlobex (getglobex.com) is a matching marketplace for US importers — not a catalog and not a trading company. The product is a short path: Match request → supplier shortlist → free matched intro. Describe your product; add the HS code if you know it.

1. Match request

You submit a free match request: a short description of your product (add the HS code if you know it), target origins if known, and a volume band. The form doesn’t store or send anything yet; once capture is on, that request starts the shortlist.

2. Supplier shortlist

We shortlist suppliers who already appear in US import records for your product, wherever they are. Real US shipping history first, not storefront ads. For public market context, see the examples on Categories: US imports of furniture & home furnishings were $57.3B and of plastic packaging & household products $29.6B (all origins, Aug 2025–Jul 2026, FOB; US-reported import statistics via UN Comtrade). That is the US import market, not GetGlobex deals.

3. Free matched intro

Free intro with the right fit. You talk samples, terms, and POs directly. We do not invent GMV, supplier counts, fit scores, or greenwash badges.

The furniture & home furnishings and plastic packaging & household products pages are examples only. Matching, not a catalog.
