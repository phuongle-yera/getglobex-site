Privacy

Draft privacy notice for the GetGlobex (getglobex.com) preview site. Nothing in the match request, seller-join, or contact stubs is stored or emailed yet.

Who we are

GetGlobex is a US import matching marketplace. We match buyers and sellers; we are not a product catalog. GetGlobex is separate from EximAgent — EximAgent is a distinct agents and data product, not a GetGlobex feature.

What this preview collects

Currently nothing via the match request, seller-join, or contact stubs. Submitting a form on this local preview only validates fields in your browser and may show an on-page draft summary. Nothing is stored on a GetGlobex server or emailed by GetGlobex.

When capture goes live

After capture is explicitly enabled, we expect to collect only what is needed to match or reply — typically:

  • Name and work email
  • Company
  • HS niche / category interest
  • Target origins (if provided)
  • Volume band
  • Seller US-lane history or readiness (for seller profiles)

We will update this notice before any live capture. We will not invent processing claims for a preview that does not yet capture data.

Cookies

Essential only on this preview (for example basic site function). No advertising or third-party analytics cookies are claimed here.

Your choices

Until capture is live, there is no account or stored profile to access or delete on GetGlobex. When capture is enabled, we will provide a clear way to ask about access, correction, or deletion of what you submitted.

Contact

Questions about this draft notice: use the Contact stub (still not stored or emailed). Or ask via your usual GetGlobex contact channel when the site is live.

Changes

We will revise this page before publish and whenever capture or processing changes. Draft site for local preview only.
