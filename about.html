About GetGlobex

We match US importers with suppliers worldwide who already ship to the US.

GetGlobex (getglobex.com) is an AI supplier-matching platform. US importers tell us the product they need. Our AI shortlists suppliers worldwide that appear in US import records for that kind of product, and we make a free intro. We are a matching service, not a trading company: we don’t buy, sell or take title to goods.

How it works

How matching works

Platform

What we are

GetGlobex is an AI supplier-matching platform for US importers and suppliers worldwide. You don’t scroll a catalog: you send a request, we shortlist from US import records, and we make a free intro. Ranking is based on fit only; nobody pays to rank higher.

What we are not

  • Not a trading company

    We don’t buy, sell or take title to goods. Buyers and suppliers deal directly.

  • Not a broker or forwarder

    We are not a customs broker or a freight forwarder.

  • Not a product catalog

    No product listings to browse and no paid placements.

  • Not a research database

    We don’t sell logins to trade data. We use it to make matches.
