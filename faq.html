Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for US importers and suppliers worldwide. AI matching marketplace — not a trading company, not a catalog.

What is GetGlobex?

GetGlobex (getglobex.com) is an AI-powered supplier matching service for US importers — not a trading company, and we don’t take title to goods. Tell us the product you need (HS code optional) and we match you with suppliers worldwide who already ship it to the US, ranked from US import records, then a free matched intro.

Is GetGlobex a broker or a forex platform?

GetGlobex (getglobex.com) is an AI supplier-matching marketplace. We are not a broker, and we don't offer forex, CFD or investment products.

Is this a catalog?

No. We match on US import records, not a catalog of product listings or paid placements.

Do you only do furniture?

No — describe any product; we match on US import records, and the HS code is optional. The furniture & home furnishings and plastic packaging & household products pages are examples only. See Categories to browse examples.

Do I need an HS code?

No. Start with the product: describe what you import or make. If you know the HS code, add it; it helps narrow the match. Want to look one up? Use the official USITC HTS search. GetGlobex does not invent an HS database.

Which origins do you cover?

Suppliers worldwide — any origin, as long as they have a US shipping history. Multi-origin here means less single-origin concentration, not “guaranteed single origin.”

Is the match request really free?

Yes. The match request and matched intro are free. We do not invent GMV, supplier counts, or timelines.

What is a free match?

A free match is the GetGlobex buyer path: you submit a match request (the product you need, the HS code if you know it, origins if known, volume band); we aim to shortlist suppliers who already appear in US import records; then we make a free matched intro. Matching, not catalog browsing.

How long does a shortlist take?

It depends on your product and origins. We won’t invent day-count SLAs or headcount.

What do I need ready?

Buyers: a short description of the product, target origins if known, and volume band or urgency. The HS code is optional (HTS lookup if you want one). Suppliers: the products you make, your origin, and US shipping history or readiness; HS code(s) optional. Our forms don’t store or email anything yet.

What do the market numbers on example pages mean?

The furniture & home furnishings and plastic packaging & household products example pages each show one figure: US import value from all origins, Aug 2025–Jul 2026, FOB, from US-reported import statistics via UN Comtrade. It describes the US import market — not GetGlobex deals, activity or platform sales. See the examples on Categories.

I’m a supplier — how do I join?

Create a free supplier profile (For suppliers: join). We send you matched buyer requests for the products you make; the HS code is optional. Ranking is based on match signals only.

Do you claim carbon-neutral or ESG scores?

No. Responsible sourcing on GetGlobex means suppliers from more than one origin and transparency on who already ships to the US — not eco badges, certified-sustainable claims, fake audits, or ESG scores.

How do I start?

Buyers: Request a free match. Suppliers: Create a free supplier profile. Or Contact us with a general question — please don’t include personal details (forms don’t store or send anything yet).
