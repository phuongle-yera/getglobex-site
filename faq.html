Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for US importers and suppliers worldwide. AI matching marketplace — not a trading company, not a catalog.

What is GetGlobex?

GetGlobex is an AI-powered supplier matching service for US importers — not a trading company, and we don’t take title to goods. We match US buyers with suppliers worldwide who already ship to the US — bring your HS — ranked from US import records, then a free matched intro.

Is GetGlobex a broker or a forex platform?

GetGlobex (getglobex.com) is an AI supplier-matching marketplace. We are not a broker, and we don't offer forex, CFD or investment products.

Is this a catalog?

No. We match on US import records, not a catalog of product listings or paid placements.

Do you only do furniture?

No — enter your HS; we match on US import records. Furniture and plastics pages are examples only. See Categories to browse examples, or look up your code on the official USITC HTS search.

What HS do I need? How do I find it?

Enter the HS code(s) for the products you import or export. Don’t know your HS? Look it up on the official USITC HTS search. GetGlobex does not invent an HS database.

Which origins do you prioritize?

Suppliers worldwide — any origin, as long as they have a US shipping history. Multi-origin here means less single-origin concentration, not “guaranteed single origin.”

Is the match request really free?

Yes. The match request and matched intro are free. We do not invent GMV, supplier counts, or timelines.

What is a free match?

A free match is the GetGlobex buyer path: you submit a match request (HS niche, origins if known, volume band); we aim to shortlist suppliers who already appear in US import records; then we make a free matched intro. Matching, not catalog browsing.

How long does a shortlist take?

It depends on your product and origins. We won’t invent day-count SLAs or headcount.

What do I need ready?

Buyers: HS code(s) (HTS lookup if needed), target origins if known, volume band or urgency. Suppliers: primary HS code(s), origin, US shipping history or readiness. Our forms don’t store or email anything yet.

What do the market numbers on example pages mean?

Example pages may show public US import market context (labeled OEC figures) and shipment activity seen in US import records over the last 12 months. These are possible matches — not closed GetGlobex deals or platform sales. See the examples on Categories.

I’m a supplier — how do I join?

Create a free supplier profile (For suppliers: join). We send you matched buyer requests in your HS. Ranking is based on match signals only.

Do you claim carbon-neutral or ESG scores?

No. Responsible sourcing on GetGlobex means resilient multi-origin optionality and lane transparency — not eco badges, certified-sustainable claims, fake audits, or ESG scores.

How do I start?

Buyers: Request a free match. Suppliers: Create a free supplier profile. Or Contact us with a general question — please don’t include personal details (forms don’t store or send anything yet).
