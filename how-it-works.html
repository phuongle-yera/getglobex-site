Buyer path

Match request → Supplier shortlist → Matched intro

Matching is the product. Bring your HS — any category. You do not browse a catalog — you submit a need, we shortlist from US import records, then make a free matched intro.

1

Match request

Buyer submits a free match request: HS code(s), optional product description, target origins if known, volume band. The form doesn’t store anything yet.

2

Supplier shortlist

We shortlist suppliers who already appear in US import records for your goods — suppliers worldwide — not storefront ads.

3

Matched intro

A free, warm intro with the right fit. You talk samples, terms, and POs directly.

AI matching, not a catalog · not a trading company. Timing depends on your product and origins — we won’t invent SLAs.

For suppliers

How suppliers get matched

Same network, other side. Create a free supplier profile; we prioritize suppliers who already ship to the US for buyer requests in your HS.

  1. Create a free supplier profile — HS, origin, US shipping history (or readiness).
  2. US shipping history first — suppliers worldwide who already ship to the US get priority in your HS.
  3. Warm match requests — sent when US buyers ask for your HS. Ranking is based on match signals only.
